Cinch is an open-source, human-in-the-loop job application assistant. It discovers roles from official job APIs, uses an LLM to tailor your master resume to each posting (rephrasing your real experience - never fabricating it), and sends the job plus the tailored resume straight to your Telegram with Approve and Skip buttons. Nothing is submitted until you tap Approve. Built to be safer than bulk auto-appliers, kinder to your accounts, and to produce better applications than spraying hundreds of generic ones.
